You bring the idea. I'm the lens that brings it into focus.

Most clients come to me with a vision and no production or design background. I take those ideas, however rough or wild, and sharpen them with what I know about camera, edit, motion and design until they're clear, watchable and hard to ignore. I plan it, shoot it, cut it, finish it, and hand over something you can actually use.

I'm a lead videographer and multimedia specialist. For 11+ years I've made training, outreach and public-facing media, working in the studio and in the field, then through edit, motion graphics, captions and delivery. I work alongside instructional designers and subject-matter experts to turn that footage into eLearning.

Most of my work serves federal missions, most recently helping the Department of Transportation reach the people who keep America's roads safe. I take that responsibility seriously.

I also vet new cameras, workflows and AI tools against cost, quality and what the shop already runs, so new gear earns its place.
